# Ali's five-minute walkthrough

This is a proposal for a prospective client, not a commissioned system. All records are fictional. Start at `/demo/` or `/dashboard/`, then show Ask → Enquire → Track. No enquiry, email, booking or file is sent to One Stop Cabinets.

| Time | Action | What to explain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:45 | Open the homepage. Click **Ask Our Kitchen Assistant**. Ask “How does your design process work?” or select a suggested question. | Customers can get a useful first answer without needing to know what to ask the sales team. The assistant shown here is scripted. |
| 0:45–1:30 | Enter “I want to start a kitchen project.” Supply fictional name/email/phone, then choose layout, budget and timing. Click **Plan my kitchen**. | Answers already collected are carried into the enquiry. Look at the selected project/layout cards and the contact step to demonstrate prefill. |
| 1:30–2:15 | For a quicker presentation, click **Try Sample Enquiry**. It fills a fictional brief and opens the review. Use **Edit** to inspect layout, preferences, contact or image preview. Click **Submit Demo Enquiry**. | A structured brief gives the business the context for a useful consultation. Budget ranges and rough measurements are not quotations. |
| 2:15–3:00 | Click **View your lead in the dashboard** on the confirmation screen. Review the project brief, references, assistant context, contact details and follow-up tasks. | The business sees the same information the customer supplied, without retyping it. This CRM is an independent proposed interface. |
| 3:00–3:45 | Click **Convert to demo project**. There is no manual status change required for the pitch. | Sales qualification connects directly to the operations workspace. A project is linked to its originating lead. |
| 3:45–4:30 | Try the **Design**, **Production** and **Installation** buttons, and tick a checklist task. Expand **Timeline, notes & settings** only if the client wants more detail. | One project can be followed through design, quotation, materials, manufacturing, quality, installation and handover. |
| 4:30–5:00 | Refresh. Show that stage, checklist and note remain. Click **View connected lead**. Open **Interactive Demo → Reset Demo Data**, confirm the local reset. | The demo's connected data persists in this browser, and all entered data can be cleared. A real system would require authenticated server storage and approved integrations. |

The compact Explore demo button opens Customer, Sales and Operations shortcuts. It stays out of the main flow on desktop and mobile. The planner groups all project details into five screens; measurements, finishing details and photos are optional expandable sections. **Start Guided Tour** explains the four experiences and navigates between them; **Explore this screen** closes the overlay so Ali can try the controls.

For an even faster demo, open `/demo/`: **View Example Lead** opens Emma Wilson's fictional qualified enquiry; **View Example Project** opens a kitchen at Manufacturing. Explore these without submitting an enquiry. Eight fictional leads and two projects are present after reset.

Submitted leads are local to the browser and origin. Opening their detail link on another device will not show that custom record; use the seeded examples when sharing the overall demo URL.
